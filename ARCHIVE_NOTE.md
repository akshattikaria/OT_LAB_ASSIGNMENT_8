This branch is a source-only archival reconstruction published after development.
Original source blobs are byte-for-byte unchanged; public commit IDs and publication timestamps differ from the original bundle.
ARCHIVE_ORIGIN.json maps each snapshot to its actual original commit/date and source hashes.
Results, large data, binaries and vendored/pristine trees are omitted from this browsing view.
The complete original history remains in the main branch: tracks/B/history.bundle.
For Track J, restore the two lossless history.bundle.parts with tools/restore_large_files.py.
These archival imports do not claim new experiments, new original-development dates, or manual student edits.
